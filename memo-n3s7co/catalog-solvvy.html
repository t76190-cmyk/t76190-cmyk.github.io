中面「系統用蓄電池 保証パッケージ」

稼働保証（最低収益を保証する新サービス）

機器不具合によるFCR※1調整力収入の減収について、需給調整市場の実績出力値80%を保証。 BMS取得データ分析サービスと年次劣化診断（EIS）の2つの手法で性能を継続的に評価 （復旧期間・収入補填の限度額は物件ごとに設定。詳細は約款の定めによる）

  • 運転データの常時解析「BMS取得データ分析サービス」：運転データ（電圧・電流・温度など）を解析し、蓄電池の状態の推移を継続的に把握します。※2
  • 年1回の精密診断「年次劣化診断（EIS）」：独自方式のEISで電池の内部状態を測定※3し、将来の劣化のKnee Pointを予測します。
  • 早稲田大学研究室発 denchi.ai と技術提携
  • ポイント：①詳細なSOH※4を測定　②劣化のKnee Pointを事前に予測

注記： ※1 FCR：需給調整市場における一次調整力。 ※2 分析の単位はBMSが取得するデータの粒度により異なります（セル単位／モジュール単位等）。 ※3 EIS測定は測定対象を受けた単位でのみ実施。拠点の設置には別途作業が必要です（対象範囲・費用は個別にご案内）。EIS（Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy）：電気化学インピーダンス測定。電池に信号を加え、応答から内部状態を評価する手法。 ※4 SOH：劣化状態（健全度）の指標。

機器保証（修理費だけでなく、現場出動から交換作業まで対応）

機器不具合が発生した場合、以下の費用を保証 - 修理や部品交換、本体の代替品交換 - 現場出動費用・現地調査費用・交換作業料

メーカーバックアップ保証：メーカーの事業撤退によってメーカー保証が受けられない場合、国内の損害保険会社または第三者機関がその保証を引き継ぎ 対象機器・部位：電池ユニット／パワーコンディショナ／BMS・EMS／その他付随する通信機器

災害補償（屋外設備の"止まる要因"を包括的にカバー）

自然災害事故による修理・交換費用を補償 - 免責金額なしのプランもご用意 保険料高騰リスクに対応した「月額プラン」 - 5年間、月額料金は据え置き - 初期費用を抑え、蓄電事業収入から月々のお支払いが可能 対象機器・部位：蓄電池ユニット／パワーコンディショナ／BMS・EMS／変圧器／受変電設備／外装設備／コンテナ

ご注意いただきたい事項（要約）：本リーフレットはサービスの概要を説明したもの。保証・補償の詳細な条件は各サービスの約款・重要事項説明書を参照。限度額・免責金額・免責期間は対象機器ごとの限度額に加えて引き受け全物件に対する年間総支払限度額の設定がある。「稼働保証」の適用範囲・保証基準・算定方法は約款の定めによる。個別案件のスキームによって責任関係が異なる場合があるため契約前に要確認。サービス内容・仕様は改善のため予告なく変更する場合がある。損害保険の商品内容は担当の保険代理店より説明を受けること。

蓄電事業者さまメリット - 蓄電事業の収益安定：稼働保証より、計画的な投資回収をサポート - 対象内の機器・災害故障は自己負担額0円：機器保証＋自然災害補償により突発的な事故が起こっても費用を気にせず現地訪問・修理対応を実現 - 蓄電池の状態を可視化：BMS取得データ分析サービスにより、蓄電池の「今」と「これから」の状態を明確に把握可能

Operating Warranty (a new service warranting minimum revenue)

Loss of FCR*1 balancing revenue caused by equipment failure is warranted up to 80% of the actual output value in the balancing market. Performance is continuously evaluated by two methods: the BMS Data Analysis Service and the Annual Degradation Diagnosis (EIS). (Recovery periods and compensation limits are set per project; details per the policy terms.)

  • Continuous analysis of operating data — "BMS Data Analysis Service": operating data (voltage, current, temperature, etc.) is analysed to continuously track the battery's state. *2
  • Annual precision diagnosis — "Annual Degradation Diagnosis (EIS)": a proprietary EIS method measures the battery's internal state *3 and predicts the future degradation Knee Point.
  • Technical partnership with denchi.ai, a Waseda University laboratory spin-off
  • Points: (1) Detailed SOH*4 measurement (2) Advance prediction of the degradation Knee Point

Notes: *1 FCR: primary balancing power in the balancing market. *2 The unit of analysis depends on the granularity of data acquired by the BMS (cell / module level, etc.). *3 EIS measurement is performed only for units that have undergone measurement; site installation requires separate work (scope and cost quoted individually). EIS (Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy): a method that applies a signal to the battery and evaluates its internal state from the response. *4 SOH: State of Health, an indicator of degradation.

Equipment Warranty (covers not only repair costs but also site visits and replacement work)

When equipment fails, the following costs are warranted: - Repair, parts replacement, and replacement of the unit itself - Site visit fees, on-site survey fees, and replacement work fees

Manufacturer back-up warranty: if the manufacturer's warranty becomes unavailable due to the manufacturer's withdrawal from the business, a domestic non-life insurer or third-party institution takes over the warranty. Covered equipment/parts: battery units / power conditioners / BMS & EMS / associated communication equipment

Disaster Compensation (comprehensive cover for outdoor equipment "stoppage factors")

Repair and replacement costs from natural disaster accidents are compensated. - Plans with no deductible are also available "Monthly Plan" addressing the risk of rising insurance premiums: - Monthly fees fixed for 5 years - Low initial cost; monthly payments can be made from battery business revenue Covered equipment/parts: battery units / power conditioners / BMS & EMS / transformers / substation equipment / exterior equipment / containers

Important notes (summary): This leaflet explains an outline of the services. For detailed warranty/compensation conditions, refer to each service's policy terms and important事項説明書. In addition to per-equipment limits, an annual aggregate payment limit applies across all accepted projects. The scope, criteria and calculation method of the Operating Warranty follow the policy terms. Liability structures may differ by project scheme, so confirmation before contracting is required. Service content and specifications may change without notice for improvement. Insurance product details are explained by the handling insurance agency.

Benefits of the Warranty

For EPC operators: - Differentiation and stronger sales: offering a proprietary warranty to battery operators differentiates you from competitors - Maximised business revenue: an additional revenue source on top of the existing battery business, aiming for greater stability - Trust in business: comprehensive warranty content removes battery operators' anxiety and concerns, contributing to customer trust

For battery operators: - Stable battery business revenue: the operating warranty supports planned investment recovery - Zero out-of-pocket cost for covered equipment and disaster failures: with equipment warranty plus natural disaster compensation, site visits and repairs can be arranged without worrying about sudden accident costs - Visualised battery state: the BMS Data Analysis Service makes the battery's "now" and "future" state clear
